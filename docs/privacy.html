Privacy Policy

WristAgent is designed around a local, user-controlled setup. The app connects your Apple Watch, iPhone, and your own Mac bridge without sending your personal data to Samboy Development.

iLast updated on June 3, 2026

Short version: WristAgent does not collect, sell, track, or share your personal data with Samboy Development. We do not operate a server that receives your messages, photos, voice, screenshots, tokens, bridge URLs, diagnostics, or agent responses.

No data collection

WristAgent does not collect data for Samboy Development.

No analytics or ads

The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, third-party tracking SDKs, remote logging, or telemetry.

Your local setup

Your iPhone talks to your own Mac bridge, typically over your private Tailscale network.

Scope

This policy explains what WristAgent and WristAgent Bridge do with data when you use the iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widget, and local Mac bridge.

WristAgent is an independent third-party companion app for compatible local agents running on a user's own Mac. It is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by the third-party agent project or Apple Inc.

Data Collection

WristAgent does not collect data for Samboy Development.

We do not receive app usage events, chat content, photos, voice recordings, transcripts, screenshots, device identifiers, location, contacts, health data, browsing history, bridge URLs, pairing secrets, diagnostics output, or agent responses.

App Store privacy summary: the app is designed so Samboy Development does not collect data from the app.

What WristAgent Communicates With

WristAgent communicates only with services and devices that you control or configure:

  • Your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework.
  • Your local WristAgent Bridge running on your Mac.
  • Your compatible local agent through the bridge running on your Mac.
  • Optional user-configured services used by your local agent setup, such as ElevenLabs for premium voice replies.

The recommended setup exposes the bridge through Tailscale Serve inside your own tailnet. Tailscale, the local agent, and ElevenLabs are separate services controlled by their own terms and privacy policies. WristAgent does not provide your credentials to Samboy Development.

What Is Stored Locally

WristAgent may store the following data locally on your devices:

  • The bridge pairing secret generated during setup, stored in the iPhone Keychain.
  • The bridge URL and diagnostics URLs, stored in local app settings.
  • Your display name, agent display name, accent color, voice settings, selected session, and other preferences.
  • Recent chat messages, response metadata, image thumbnails, and local message state inside the iPhone and Apple Watch app sandboxes.
  • Temporary audio files used to play voice replies on Apple Watch.
  • Temporary image files or thumbnails needed to display screenshots or photo attachments.

This local data is used to make pairing, messaging, session switching, image display, and voice playback work across launches.

Photos, Speech, and Voice

Photos

WristAgent may request Photo Library access on iPhone when you choose to attach a photo to a Watch message or preview recent photos.

When you use this feature, the app reads the selected or recent photo locally, prepares a Watch-sized preview or an attachment payload, and sends it through your configured private path: iPhone to your Mac bridge to your compatible local agent.

WristAgent does not send photos to Samboy Development. If your own local agent setup forwards images to an AI provider, that behavior is controlled by your local agent configuration and that provider's terms.

Speech Recognition

WristAgent may request Speech Recognition permission only when you dictate a message from Apple Watch or iPhone-supported flows.

Speech transcription uses Apple's speech recognition capabilities. WristAgent does not send your voice recordings or transcripts to Samboy Development.

Voice Replies

By default, Watch voice replies use Apple's speech synthesis. If you enable ElevenLabs voice and your local agent setup is configured for it, audio responses may be generated through ElevenLabs by your Mac/local agent setup. WristAgent itself does not store or transmit your ElevenLabs API key to Samboy Development.

Pairing Tokens and Security

The WristAgent Bridge setup generates a pairing secret used to authenticate requests from your iPhone/Watch to your Mac bridge.

  • The token is stored in the iPhone Keychain.
  • Pairing files generated by WristAgent Bridge may contain the token.
  • Pairing files should stay private and should not be uploaded, published, or shared.
  • If a pairing token is exposed, regenerate the pairing configuration from the bridge setup.

The bridge binds to 127.0.0.1 by default and is intended to be exposed privately through Tailscale Serve. Public internet exposure is not part of the default setup.

Data Retention and Deletion

WristAgent stores app data locally for as long as the app remains installed or until you reset or re-pair your configuration.

You can remove locally stored WristAgent data by:

  • Deleting WristAgent from iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Clearing or regenerating your local bridge configuration on the Mac.
  • Deleting generated bridge files under ~/.openclaw/openclaw-watch/ if you no longer need them. This directory name is retained for compatibility with existing bridge installs.

Because Samboy Development does not collect or host your WristAgent data, there is no Samboy Development server-side account or cloud database to delete.

Children

WristAgent is intended as a productivity utility and does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.
